Performance Build PB-2: TimePitchProcessor abstraction

Introduces the replaceable time/pitch abstraction the Player uses:

- TimePitchProcessor trait: tempo_ratio, pitch_semitones, latency_frames,
  set_source/next_frame/position/seek/reset. The player never talks to a
  specific stretching algorithm.
- VarispeedProcessor: first implementation. Catmull-Rom cubic interpolation
  (replaces integer truncation), pitch coupled to tempo plus independent
  semitone shift compounding the read rate. Zero latency.
- Player delegates all source positioning to the processor. Loop/seek logic
  queries processor.position_frames() and processor.seek_frames().
- New EngineCommand::SetPitch + Tauri command audio_engine_set_pitch.
- 5 processor unit tests: unity reproduction, 2x tempo exactness, pitch
  compounding, seek/reset, half-frame interpolation.

A pitch-preserving Master Tempo stretcher can now drop in behind the same
trait without touching the engine.
